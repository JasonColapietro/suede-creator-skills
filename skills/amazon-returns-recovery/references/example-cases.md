# Synthetic example cases

These fictional scenarios illustrate the workflow. They are not customer records,
transcripts, or evidence of actual recoveries. Names, account details, dates, and
transaction amounts are intentionally omitted. Replace placeholders only with the
account owner's confirmed facts in their private session.

## Case 1: Request a restocking-fee waiver

- **Situation:** a completed return has a restocking-fee deduction.
- **Ask:** request a courtesy waiver for the specific fee shown in the refund breakdown.
- **Illustrative response:** the associate promises a waiver to the original payment method.
- **Report:** mark the amount as promised until the posted refund confirms it.

## Case 2: Clarify a different refund amount

- **Situation:** the associate offers an amount different from the requested waiver.
- **Ask:** clarify which charges the offer covers and how it will be refunded.
- **Illustrative response:** the associate confirms the components and expected timing.
- **Report:** record the confirmed terms privately; verify the posted amount later.

## Case 3: Request review of a denied refund

- **Situation:** a refund request was denied and the return window has closed.
- **Ask:** if the account owner's actual facts support it, request a one-time exception.
- **Illustrative response:** the associate offers a partial refund or declines.
- **Next step:** make at most one polite counter with a truthful reason, then report the outcome.

## Applying the examples

- Confirm the seller and the user's requested outcome before contacting support.
- State only facts supplied by the account owner or shown in their account.
- Never reuse an example as a claim that a real customer experienced it.
- Keep orders, household details, personal reasons, and support transcripts in the
  user's private session. Do not add them to this skill, a public report, or a repository.
- Quote actual support confirmation only in the private report to that account owner.
- A promise is not a posted refund, and no example guarantees recovery.
