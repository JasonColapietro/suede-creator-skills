# Subject Profiles: What Changes per Company, Local Business, Person, or Creator

The ladder is the same for everyone. What changes is the money query, the
proof, and which levels carry the most weight. Pick the profile (or blend two)
during diagnosis.

| Profile | Money query looks like | Heavy levels | Proof that wins | Common trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SaaS / B2B company | "[category] software", "[competitor] alternatives", "how to [job]" | L30, L40, L60, L80 | screenshots, pricing, comparisons, customer logos with permission, original data | blog traffic that never touches product pages |
| E-commerce / DTC brand | "[product type] for [use]", "best [product]", "[brand] [product]" | L40 (product/category pages), L60, L80 | real photos, reviews, specs, shipping/returns | duplicate product descriptions from suppliers; faceted URL bloat |
| Local service business | "[service] [city]", "[service] near me" | L20 (GBP), L40 (service + location pages), L80 (reviews, local links) | Google reviews, real job photos, service area, licenses | one generic page for all services; inconsistent NAP |
| Person / personal brand / founder / expert | "[full name]", "[name] [role]", topic queries they speak on | L20 (name SERP, knowledge panel), L80 (podcasts, talks, bylines), L90 | bio with credentials, talks, publications, media | a name shared with someone more famous: add a qualifier (role, city) everywhere |
| Creator / artist / band | "[artist name]", "[artist] [song/album]", "[genre] artist [city]" | L20, L40 (release and bio pages), L90 | discography, press, tour dates (`MusicGroup`, `Event` schema), official links | streaming profiles outrank the official site; no single canonical bio |
| Publisher / media / newsletter | topic queries at scale | L30, L60, L70, L100 | original reporting, authors with bylines | thin pages at scale; title tests skipped |
| Nonprofit / public org | "[cause] [city]", "how to help [cause]", program names | L20, L40, L80 (.edu/.gov/partner links) | impact numbers, programs, transparency | donation page not indexable |

## Notes per profile

**Person.** Most people stall at L20. Fix the name SERP first: a personal site
whose title leads with the exact name, a `Person` schema with `sameAs`, and a
consistent one-line bio on every profile. If the name is common, pick a
qualifier and use it everywhere ("Maria Ortiz, Austin wedding photographer").
Knowledge panels come from consistent entity data plus real mentions (press,
podcasts, talks), not from a form.

**Local.** Google Business Profile drives the map pack, which sits above organic
results. Separate service pages (and real location pages only where there is
a genuine presence or service area) beat one catch-all page. Reviews: ask every
happy client, reply to every review, never buy or gate them.

**New company with no data.** Skip straight past L60 in planning only; the title
loop starts once GSC shows impressions (usually 2-6 weeks after indexing).
Until then the 14-day plan is L0-L40 work.

**Established company.** Usually sits at L40-L50 with gaps lower down
(cannibalization, missing schema, weak name SERP for a renamed brand).
Audit lower gates quickly, then run L60 at full cohort volume.

**Artist / creator.** Pair with `suede-campaign-in-a-box` for release moments;
each release gets its own indexable page with lyrics excerpt rules respected,
credits, and links, so the official site, not a lyrics aggregator, owns the
release query.
