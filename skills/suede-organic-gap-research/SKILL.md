# Suede Organic Gap Research Skills

## Compatibility

Use authorized Ahrefs or equivalent data, a supplied export, or a deliberately
small public evidence set. Confirm provider budget and scope before live paid
calls. Mixed provider results must be labeled, never silently combined.

## Procedure

1. Choose direct commercial competitors separately from search-result
   competitors. Match geography, market, device and date window. Record the
   provider, export time, definitions, and canonical scope for every dataset.
   Review leading page types and recent availability. Traffic estimates do not
   prove sessions; domain scores do not prove link value.
2. Compare competitor landing pages and query groups against your own
   inventory. Identify whether readers already have a suitable owned page.
   Distinguish a missing page from a refresh, a consolidation, or a question
   outside the product. Validate candidate priorities against corresponding
   search results and your actual product fit before assigning them.
3. Normalize referring domains and validate source pages rather than counting
   all backlinks. Identify the editorial reason for the citation, the last
   verified URL and link state, the actual target, reader audience, and which
   owned asset could legitimately earn a mention. Deduplicate repeat instances;
   discard junk, claims of guaranteed placement, and pay-for-link offers.
4. Rank high-confidence opportunities by relevance, evidenced demand and
   feasibility. Draft implementation tasks with a specific owner, evidence
   pointer, acceptance check, and decision that still needs review. Link
   prospects are leads to assess, not permission to email or buy placement.

## Output contract

Provide a content matrix (candidate query, comparable evidence, existing
URL, action, reason) and an optional backlink evidence CSV (source URL,
referring domain, competitor target, citation context, suggested owned fit,
last checked). Label what is sampled, export-based, confirmed, or missing.
Do not publish new pages or send outreach without authorization.

## Boundaries

- Treat provider metrics as estimates, not demand or placement guarantees.
- Do not spend credits, solicit links, or contact prospects without authorization.
