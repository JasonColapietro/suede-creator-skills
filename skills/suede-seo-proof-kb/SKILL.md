# Suede SEO Proof Library Skills

A published claim should have a stable identifier and one inspectable record. Keep raw private evidence in its approved location; store a reference, not a hidden duplicate.

## Evidence record

Record: claim ID; proposed public language; source owner and evidence URL or private handle; observed date and applicable window; method, population, sample, and exclusions; result and comparison baseline; consent and permitted audience; expiry or next check; reviewer; pages and anchors using the claim. Mark the record verified, unverified, stale, revoked, or internal-only. A metric from one client or run is never a general guarantee.

- For a benchmark, preserve inputs, measurement method, environment, the raw result reference and known outliers.
- For customer evidence, distinguish a direct approved quote from the team's own summary. Do not name, quote or link a private customer without permission.
- For capability, price or compatibility facts, cite the precise source of truth and check scope restrictions before reuse.
- If a claim lacks evidence, propose an interview question or measurement. Do not create a placeholder quotation or estimate as fact.

## Apply the library to a page

Map each important promise to a verified, public-eligible claim; tell readers what was measured and why it matters. Attach the smallest supporting detail that clarifies the decision. If evidence is missing, use a narrower supported claim or leave the assertion out.

When sourcing answers for an AI-search prompt gap, record the panel date, markets and models, prompts actually completed, existing owned URL, and the claim IDs that could substantiate a useful answer. Distinguish missing evidence from missing content. Prefer refreshing the URL that already answers the intent over opening another page for a wording variant.

On corrections or revocation, list every impacted URL and snippet (including comparison tables and metadata). Give an owner the replacement or removal text and then confirm the live output when authorized. Return the ledger delta, public-safe excerpts and the impact list; never disclose private evidence or publish from this skill.

## Boundaries

- Exclude confidential or unapproved examples from outputs intended for readers.
- Mark stale, disputed, or unsupported evidence instead of turning it into a claim.
