# Worked delivery contract

This synthetic scenario demonstrates the expected artifact shape; its numbers and outcomes are example inputs, not measured customer results.

## Input

A lead campaign spent 2,000 currency units for 40 leads. Sales closes 20% of leads and contribution before acquisition is 400 per customer. All amounts use the same currency and reporting window.

## Expected output

Report observed CPL 50, modeled customer acquisition cost 250 and break-even CPL 80 (400 × 0.20). State that the close-rate assumption must describe the same cohort. Propose a bounded test with a spend cap, conversion lag, review window and stop condition; do not treat this calculation as authority to increase spend.

## Failure case

The 20% close rate is an all-time average and current leads have not matured. Present the scenario calculation separately from observed performance and keep the budget decision provisional pending cohort evidence.

## Validation

Reconcile spend/lead totals to the export, check currency and conversion windows, and compare mature qualified-lead cohorts. Verify live platform controls before proposing exact setting names; report account changes only after readback.
