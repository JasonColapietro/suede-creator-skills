# Worked delivery contract

This synthetic scenario demonstrates the expected artifact shape; its numbers and outcomes are example inputs, not measured customer results.

## Input

An inventory app supports barcode intake and low-stock alerts. Produce one Google RSA concept for warehouse managers from those two verified capabilities. No testimonial, quantified savings or offer deadline is supplied.

## Expected output

Return the angle and source-to-claim map, then the requested RSA assets with individual character counts and combination review. Example safe headline: Barcode Inventory (17 characters). Example description: Track stock with barcode intake and low-stock alerts. (53 characters). Expand to the requested complete asset set with distinct truthful benefits; these two sample strings alone are not a complete RSA.

## Failure case

The brief asks for a customer quote without a source. Keep the customer-proof slot open and offer feature-based copy; never manufacture a review. If a media tool is unavailable, return the storyboard/export specification and identify rendering as pending.

## Validation

Count characters on the exact final strings, inspect arbitrary headline combinations for repetition/contradiction, and confirm every claim appears in the provided source. Verify the current platform preview before launch.
