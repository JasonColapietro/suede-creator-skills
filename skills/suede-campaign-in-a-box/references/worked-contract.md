# Worked delivery contract

This synthetic scenario demonstrates the expected artifact shape; its numbers and outcomes are example inputs, not measured customer results.

## Input

An artist owns a new single and cover artwork. Release is in four weeks. Budget covers owned channels only; no collaborator permission or venue agreement is supplied.

## Expected output

Choose the relevant lane and return an announce/tease/release-week/post-drop calendar with asset owner, source, exact fan action and review status per item. Separate owned artwork from collaborator and venue material whose use is unresolved. Produce a reviewable campaign packet; do not imply posts are scheduled.

## Failure case

An attractive live-performance clip lacks permission from its owner. Flag that asset and continue with the cleared song/artwork; route the rights gap rather than quietly substituting a public download.

## Validation

Match all titles/dates/credits to the release record, inspect links and clear each asset separately. Distinguish draft, approved and published states, and measure the named fan action rather than assuming reach means demand.
