Suede AI Clip to Guide Skills Make the clip the door, not the destination.

A clip has to score 8 of 10 on the moment gate before it earns a guide. Third-party footage runs a rights route that can end in blocked, with the fair-use guidance cited and no clearance claimed. Then the exact bridge copy from the clip to the long-form piece and a verified publishing sequence. Video production itself is suede-video.

View skill folder Install options

When to reach for it

Use it for a video-plus-guide post, X Article promo, LinkedIn article or newsletter bridge, clip-to-blog campaign, or any short-to-long funnel where the deeper written asset answers the question the clip creates.

What's inside

  • Default success schematic and fit criteria
  • Original, licensed, native-repost, fair-use-review, and blocked rights routes
  • Five-part, 10-point video-moment score
  • Guide-anchor and claim-support gates
  • Clip brief, exact funnel post, and verified publish sequence
  • Two distinct proof checks in max-effort or worker-fleet mode
  • Exact-content and visible-identity approval bundle
  • Durable Markdown package and validator script
  • Clip-to-guide measurement and one-variable iteration
