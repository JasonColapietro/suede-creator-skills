/suede-rights-audit · Rights gap audit

Find the ownership gaps before someone else does.

Use Suede Rights Audit before a creator project gets packaged to find missing owners, unconfirmed splits, unclear samples, provenance gaps, metadata gaps, public URLs, licensing-clearance readiness, royalty-routing readiness, and intake blockers. It flags confirmed versus unknown facts across ownership, contributors, credits, licenses, provenance, and payment routing so rights passporting, licensing prep, registry readiness, or routing review starts from evidence instead of inference.

What it covers

  • Rights-gap audits across ownership, contributors, credits, splits, samples, licenses, provenance, and public context.
  • A shared evidence table with status, evidence, path or hash, risk, blockers, and next action for each item.
  • Provenance maps for sessions, masters, stems, artwork, lyrics, documents, metadata files, public URLs, hashes, conflicts, and do-not-share items.
  • Licensing-discussion readiness briefs that separate confirmed facts, open questions, restrictions, unsafe claims removed, and next steps.
  • Royalty-routing readiness summaries for contributor roles, split percentages, payment destinations, unresolved parties, and payout caveats.
  • Mechanical ship gates: blocked for high-risk items, unknown for unknown risk or status, and ready-for-review when no blockers remain.

When to use it

  • Before rights passporting, provenance mapping, licensing prep, registry readiness, royalty routing, payment-destination layout, or Suede creator intake.
  • Before a sync, brand, or partner licensing discussion where clearance gaps must stay visible.
  • When a project has thin source files, unclear samples, missing contributors, disputed splits, or public URLs that need review notes.
  • When the user needs confirmed facts, missing facts, blockers, creator questions, safe public wording, and the next lane or skill.
  • Not for: building the transfer package itself (use suede-rights-passport).
  • Not for: linting release folders for files and metadata (use suede-release-linter).
  • Not for: sync one-sheets (use suede-sync-packaging).

Best prompt

Use $suede-rights-audit to run a rights-gap audit and provenance map on this creator project, separate confirmed facts from unknowns, flag blockers, list questions for the creator, and give safe public wording plus the next lane or skill.

Related skills

Use Suede Rights Passport when the gaps are organized and the user wants the package. Use Suede Release Linter for folder, file, and metadata lint before or after the audit.

Safety boundary

This skill finds and organizes rights gaps as local evidence. It does not clear rights, confirm ownership, adjudicate chain of title, grant or imply a license, approve or schedule a payout, move money, write to any registry, or provide legal advice.
