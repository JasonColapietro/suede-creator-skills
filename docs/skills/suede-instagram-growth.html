What it owns

One Instagram operating system, seven concrete jobs.

Account audit

Recent-post pattern map

Codes up to 30 posts by format, topic, hook, proof, CTA, production burden, and available performance evidence.

Conversion

Views are not sales

Classifies posts as converts, assists, attention-only, or unknown without inventing private metrics or competitor outcomes.

Reels

Timed scripts and shot plans

Delivers scored hooks, second-by-second beats, faceless production options, caption, CTA, rights checks, and a test card.

Carousels and Stories

Exact copy, one job per frame

Selects the narrative first, then writes every slide or frame with visual direction and one earned next action.

Calendar

Experiments instead of filler

Every calendar row names the audience problem, evidence source, one variable, primary metric, owner, approval state, and readback field.

Daily loop

Candidates before publishing

Refreshes account and trend evidence, scores 1–3 candidates, completes QA, and returns the exact approval packet before any external action.

Evidence model

No private metric gets reverse-engineered from a public view count.

Observed-publicVisible post, caption, date, format, views, likes, and comments.
Observed-ownedAuthorized Insights, export, link analytics, CRM, DM, and checkout evidence.
ComputedA named formula with its denominator and source values shown.
InferredA hypothesis with a test, never phrased as account truth.
UnknownThe measure is absent and the resolving evidence is named.

Daily loop

Refresh, select, produce, prove.

Refresh

Read the account brief, prior readback, active offer, audience signals, official guidance, and cleared media library.

Select

Score candidates on audience recognition, payoff, evidence, voice fit, and business bridge.

Produce

Build the full format package, verify rights and claims, then show the exact approval packet.

Prove

After explicit approval, read back the live permalink, identity, media, caption, tags, CTA, and measurement checkpoint.

Boundaries

Growth without account theater.

  • No unauthorized scraping, password collection, access-control bypass, or invented Insights.
  • No publish, schedule, comment, follow, like, repost, or DM action without exact-content and visible-identity approval.
  • No guaranteed virality, fixed hashtag quota, universal best time, fake social proof, engagement pods, or purchased followers.
  • No third-party music, footage, voice, likeness, logo, testimonial, or creator post without a verified lawful basis.
  • No "published" claim until the live permalink and rendered content are read back.
