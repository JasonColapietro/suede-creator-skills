/suede-deslop · Copy & writing; the pack's anti-slop pass

Cut the slop before it ships.

AI prose has tells: throat-clearing before the point, inanimate things doing human work, contrasts that announce the insight instead of delivering it, rhythm that never varies. This skill runs your text against a merged kill list, unwinds the formulaic structures, forces active voice, then scores what remains 1-10 on directness, rhythm, trust, authenticity, and density. Below 35 of 50 it says revise. It edits style only: facts, numbers, names, and claims stay exactly as you wrote them.

The eight rules

Every pass runs the same eight rules, then a 16-item pre-ship checklist. The high-frequency offenders live in the skill body; the full sweep, forty-plus more phrases and every structure variant, ships in the bundled references/kill-list.md.

RuleWhat it kills
Cut filler phrasesThroat-clearing openers, emphasis crutches, business jargon, every adverb
Break formulaic structuresBinary contrasts, negative listing, dramatic fragments, rhetorical setups, false agency
Active voice, every sentencePassive constructions and inanimate things doing human work
Be specificVague declaratives and lazy extremes: every, always, never, everyone, nobody
Put the reader in the roomNarrator-from-a-distance voice; "you" beats "people"
Vary rhythmMetronomic sentence lengths, triad lists, punchy endings, em dashes
Trust the readerSoftening, justification, hand-holding
Cut quotablesSentences written to be screenshotted

The score gate

After the pass, the text gets scored 1-10 on five dimensions. Below 35 of 50 the verdict is REVISE, with a named reason for what still generates the score.

DimensionQuestion
DirectnessStatements, not announcements?
RhythmVaried, not metronomic?
TrustRespects the reader?
AuthenticitySounds human?
DensityAnything still cuttable?

What it covers

  • A merged kill list: 19 throat-clearing openers, 18 jargon swaps, 24 named adverbs plus a blanket no-adverb rule, meta-commentary, performative sincerity, and vague declaratives.
  • Every binary-contrast spelling, negative listing, reveal fragments, formulaic templates, permission grants, and lecturer-voice tells, each with its fix.
  • A 16-item pre-ship checklist and a counted report: filler removed, structures fixed, passive flipped, em dashes cut.
  • Style edits only. A rewrite that needs a specific the source lacks gets an [AUTHOR: supply X] placeholder, never an invented fact.
  • Returns cleaned prose in the response; the author decides where it lands. It never publishes, posts, or overwrites the original.

When to use it

  • Copy, a README, an email, a social post, or a doc is about to ship.
  • After a long AI-assisted writing session.
  • The text sounds fine but feels generated.
  • Before anything goes to press, investors, or customers.
  • NOT for writing new copy from scratch. Use suede-copy.
  • NOT for verifying facts or claims. Factual wording needs primary evidence before publication.

Best prompt

Use $suede-deslop to clean [paste text]. Return the cleaned prose, the counted deslop report, and the 50-point score. Never change a fact; mark missing specifics with [AUTHOR: supply X].
