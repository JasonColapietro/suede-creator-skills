/suede-prospecting · Marketing & growth

Suede AI Prospecting Skills The list is built by cutting. Disqualify before you qualify.

It names who is not a customer before it names who is, and writes the disqualification evidence down. Then ICP fit, sourcing, enrichment, and account scoring on a bounded list, including where the early adopters and design partners are. Sending the outreach is suede-cold-email; CRM routing is suede-revops.
