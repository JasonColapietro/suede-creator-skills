cracked stack post · technical 
I added a curated stack to Suede Creator Skills and made the marketing do engineering work.

The Agentic Adderall Stack groups the pack's six orchestration skills. Instant release is one continuous subagent-driven pass: the controller fans every disjoint lane out at once, nothing waits on a timer, adversarial review runs inside the pass, and the close is an evidence gate. Extended release is the scheduling counterpart: a scorer ranks the next move on goal fit, unblocking, evidence, urgency, and leverage, then emits it as a runnable prompt.

The grouping is a cross-cutting overlay in the catalog, not a seventh category. The specialty partition stays total, the validator fails CI if the stack's page and its catalog membership drift, and the MCP server scopes the stack per profile. Cost and authority stay on the label: suede-ship's Graph-of-Thoughts search generates competing plans, caps light/standard/deep at 55/110/200 calls, and lets only the selected winner mutate. Production inspection is read-only and it never deploys. The fleet skill routes bulk volume to Codex CLI workers billed outside the Anthropic meter.

Safety copy

  • Public v1 runs offline and writes local files.
  • Generated reports are drafts until a creator or operator reviews them.
  • These skills organize evidence. They do not provide legal clearance.
  • The scripts skip hidden files, dependency folders, build outputs, caches, and secret-like files by default.
  • Unknown rights facts stay marked as unknown.
