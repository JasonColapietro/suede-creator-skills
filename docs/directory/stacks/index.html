How a stack is chosen

A stack is chosen from the directory's snapshot: skills that match the task, fit together, carry full pages and score well on the Suede Skill Check, with install counts from skills.sh where they exist. The Suede AI skill named above each article is kept out of the ranking and out of the article body. Stacks are reviewed after each directory refresh.
