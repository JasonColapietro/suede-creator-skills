How to choose market research skills

Check where the data comes from. Some market research skills drive a search or scraping service such as Firecrawl and need its API key; others work from what you paste in.

Pick a skill whose output matches the decision you face: a one-off market report, a recurring competitor watch, or positioning notes for a product team.

Read the sources a report cites before you rely on its numbers. The skill structures the research; the facts still come from the pages it reads.
