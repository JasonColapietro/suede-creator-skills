How to choose scriptwriting skills

write-video-ad-script plans words, product proof and shots for a short ad; adaptation converts a story into screenplay (Fountain), audio narration or picture-book formats; blog-repurpose turns a post into a script among other formats.

These skills need no credentials. Pair a script with a video or voice skill when you want the agent to produce the finished asset.

Read scripts aloud before recording. Timing that looks right on the page often runs long once spoken.
