How to choose HR and recruiting skills

Candidate skills mostly come as a set: resume tailoring, bullet rewriting, ATS checks, cover letters, interview prep and offer comparison. Install the set and keep one master resume as the input every skill reads.

Hiring-side skills such as the CV scorer and Culture Index interpreter evaluate people. Use them to structure a review against the job description, and keep the hiring decision with a person on your team.

Job search and application skills browse job boards and fill Greenhouse, Lever or Workday forms. Check the tool permissions on each page, and review every answer before a form is submitted.
