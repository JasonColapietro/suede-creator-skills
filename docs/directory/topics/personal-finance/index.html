How to choose personal finance skills

Decide between a local workbook and a connected account. The budget builder writes a local Excel file; portfolio trackers call a provider's API and read its key.

Crypto skills that can act on a wallet are review-first by design; read their SKILL.md and keep any signing keys out of the agent's environment.

Treat optimization and backtest output as analysis. Decisions about real money stay with you or a licensed advisor.
