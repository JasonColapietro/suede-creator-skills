How to choose marketing strategy skills

For a full plan, start with marketing-plan, suede-marketing-plan or one-page-marketing. For a launch, use launch or gtm-0-to-1-launch plus a checklist skill. For a single hard call, a council skill gives several expert perspectives in one pass.

These are reasoning skills and need no credentials. Their output is only as good as the product marketing context you give them, so keep a current positioning and ICP document and point each skill at it.

Prefer skills that end with a prioritized shortlist and a 30, 60 or 90 day schedule over open-ended idea lists. A ranked plan with owners is the part you can act on.
