How to choose AI detection and humanizing skills

Separate detection from rewriting. ai-writing-detector and false-positive-reviewer score and explain flags without touching your text; avoid-ai-writing, unslop, stop-slop and suede-deslop rewrite it.

Check how a rewriter protects content. The stronger skills preserve code, quotes, links, frontmatter and facts and verify that afterward; HumanPen rewrites .docx and .pptx files through its hosted service, which needs an account.

No detector proves authorship. Use these skills to improve clarity and voice, and read flagged passages yourself before changing them.
