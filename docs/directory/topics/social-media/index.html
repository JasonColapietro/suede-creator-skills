How to choose social media skills

Match the skill to the platform. The linkedin-skills collection covers posts, comments, replies, profiles and employee advocacy in depth; goose-skills splits drafting (create-x-content, create-linkedin-content) from research (social-listening, trend-discovery, outlier-post-finder).

Research skills that read live profiles, search TikTok or monitor mentions call third-party scrapers or APIs and list the key they need. Writing skills work from a brief and, in several cases, a voice guide you generate once and reuse.

Keep publishing in your own hands. Schedulers such as timetopost-social-scheduler stop for approval of the exact post and time, which is the pattern to look for in any skill that touches a live account.
