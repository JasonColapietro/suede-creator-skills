How to choose legal skills

Use drafting skills for a first version. The NDA and privacy policy skills ask for parties, jurisdiction and data types, then produce a full draft with the clauses that need a decision marked for review.

Engineering compliance skills such as the GDPR and API compliance checkers work on your codebase. They are most useful during design and code review, when a data flow or log schema is still cheap to change.

Standards readiness skills organize evidence against named frameworks such as ISO 13485 and ISO 14971. Give them your actual procedures and records, and use their gap list to plan the audit.
