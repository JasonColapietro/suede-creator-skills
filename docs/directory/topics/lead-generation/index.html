How to choose lead generation skills

Decide between inbound and outbound. Inbound skills (lead-magnets, free-tools, scorecard-marketing, popups, signup) shape what you offer on your own site; prospecting skills (lead-discovery, firecrawl-lead-gen) find people and companies to contact and need that data provider's key.

Ask each skill how it qualifies a lead. The stronger ones define the qualifying signal up front and connect the asset to a paid offer, which keeps a list from filling with contacts who never buy.

Pair this topic with an email skill. A lead magnet or free tool only pays off when the follow-up sequence is ready on the day it launches.
