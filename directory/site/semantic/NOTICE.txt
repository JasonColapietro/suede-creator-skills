Suede semantic search uses MinishLab's pretrained potion-base-2M Model2Vec
embedding model. Its tokenizer and 64-dimensional embedding parameters come
from revision 389b9f64be5aa4ae7a6bc6fe95ef20ce485ae5da, designated MIT by
its publisher. Suede repackaged the tokenizer and quantized each learned
embedding row to 8-bit integers for local browser inference.

Model card and license designation:
