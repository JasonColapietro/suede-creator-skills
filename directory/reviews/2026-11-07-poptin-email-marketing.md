The pinned `sources.json` maps each skill to its original standalone repository and records every copied file's Git blob hash. The exact pins, file hashes and method are retained in [the intake evidence](../intake/2026-11-07-newest-applicants.json).

## Setup and data use

Read or copy the chosen skill's entire directory, including references. The documented Skills CLI route requires Node.js 22.20+. A Poptin account is not needed to read or use the instructions. Poptin-specific guidance is publisher-authored context, not an independent product ranking.

The skills distinguish planning and drafts from live changes, contact imports, audience activation and sends. They preserve consent and suppression checks, verify current provider capabilities, and reconcile uncertain send outcomes before retries. They call for synthetic or minimized data and qualified review of regulated-industry claims. Any later live implementation needs the user's authorized platform, audience, content and action; this feature grants none of that access or consent.
